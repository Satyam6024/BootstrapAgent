# BootstrapAgent

**Plan. Build. Launch. Automate.**

BootstrapAgent is a technology and product-building initiative focused on using **AI, software, automation, and agentic systems** to turn ideas into working products.

We are building from zero, with a simple philosophy:

> **Build first. Learn fast. Ship continuously.**

## What We Do

BootstrapAgent operates at the intersection of:

* **Artificial Intelligence** — LLMs, RAG, AI agents, and intelligent workflows
* **Software Engineering** — web applications, APIs, platforms, and developer tools
* **Automation** — replacing repetitive manual workflows with reliable systems
* **Product Development** — validating ideas and turning them into usable products
* **Startup Building** — experimenting with businesses without depending on large teams or outside funding

## Our Approach

We believe a small, capable team can build surprisingly ambitious products when the right tools and processes are used.

Our operating loop is:

```text
IDEA
  ↓
RESEARCH
  ↓
PROTOTYPE
  ↓
VALIDATE
  ↓
BUILD
  ↓
SHIP
  ↓
MEASURE
  ↓
ITERATE
```

We use AI wherever it creates a real advantage, but we do not treat AI as a substitute for engineering judgment. **Data quality, system design, evaluation, reliability, and user feedback still matter.**

## Current Focus

Our work includes experimentation with:

* AI agents and agentic workflows
* Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
* AI-powered knowledge systems
* Automation platforms
* Developer tools
* SaaS products
* Internal productivity systems
* Startup experiments

One example is **CementGPT**, an agentic RAG system designed to help users query large collections of cement-industry documentation and retrieve actionable, source-grounded information.

## The Team

BootstrapAgent is intentionally being built as a **lean, execution-focused team**.

We are interested in people who:

* Build instead of only discussing ideas
* Are comfortable learning unfamiliar technologies
* Take ownership of problems
* Communicate clearly
* Experiment quickly
* Care about shipping useful products

You do **not** need to be an expert or have a particular degree.

A strong contributor can come from software engineering, AI/ML, research, design, product, growth, operations, or another discipline that helps us build.

## Our Principles

### Build Before Perfect

A working prototype teaches more than weeks of speculation.

### Own the Outcome

We care about whether something works, not whether someone completed their assigned task.

### Use AI Pragmatically

AI should reduce cost, increase speed, or improve capability. If it does none of those things, we do not need it.

### Stay Lean

We prefer small teams, simple systems, and low operating costs.

### Measure Reality

Opinions are useful for generating hypotheses. Users and data decide whether those hypotheses survive.

## Why "BootstrapAgent"?

**Bootstrap** represents building with limited resources, starting from zero, and creating momentum through execution rather than relying on large amounts of outside capital.

**Agent** represents our technical direction: AI agents, automation, software systems, and tools that can perform useful work.

Together:

> **BootstrapAgent = building ambitious technology with a lean, AI-native approach.**
